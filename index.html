Tally Excel Sync

Upload the Tally export. The MIS stores the imported rows permanently and processes only NEW, uniquely matched dispatches.

What happens after Import?
1. The Excel rows are saved in TallySales.
2. Already-imported identical rows are ignored.
3. A unique Tally row is matched to an MIS order using Order No + Part Code, then safe fallback matching.
4. A successful match updates Dispatch Qty, Dispatch History and FG Stock.
5. Unmatched or changed rows go to TallyExceptions and do not change stock automatically.
